# Math 1202 Review

A generated practice question bank for **Mathematics 1202** (Applied stream, Newfoundland and Labrador), built on the same chassis as the Math 7, 8, 9, 1201, 2200, 2201, 3200, 3201, 3202, and 3208 Review apps, with the junior scaffolding set: staged hints, worked examples, and immediate feedback.

**Live app:** https://cdli-nlschools.github.io/Math1202Review/ (once GitHub Pages is enabled)

## What it does

- Opens on a simple student face: the selected scope, one line about what will happen, and one large start button. The full builder sits collapsed under "Build your own practice". If no units are chosen, the start button opens the builder and scrolls to it.
- Draws fresh practice questions by unit or by curriculum indicator. The bank holds 182 entries covering all 96 curriculum indicators across the seven units. Some indicators are placed in more than one unit by the official spreadsheet (the formula indicators 2.1 to 2.6 recur across units, and several measurement indicators repeat between Units 1 and 2): each indicator gets its full treatment in its home unit, and the other units carry entries set in that unit's own contexts.
- No mid-course or final exam presets: this course has none. Instead, each unit has a practice test modelled on the development shell's unit tests (the documented Unit 1 test draws 15 selected-response questions at 1 mark each and 6 constructed-response questions at 2 to 3 marks from variant groups of three, 30 points): 15 selected-response questions with a 3/9/3 level split, plus 6 constructed-response questions (three at 2 marks and three at 3 marks, with at least one at Level 3), for 30 marks in every unit. Practice tests are built one unit at a time; asking for one across several units returns guidance instead of a paper.
- Practice tests offer an optional soft timer modelled on a 1 hour sitting (a single setting, TEST_MINUTES, drives the label). It counts up, never locks or submits anything. Note: the development shell's own unit tests are set to 120 minutes; the 1 hour model is the project decision and is flagged as item 1 on the QA spot-check list.
- Three ways to work: practice online with check-as-you-go marking, print a practice sheet (with working space and an optional answer key), or print a unit practice test with a separate teacher key. Sheets and tests can also be downloaded as a Word file with native editable equations, or copied for Google Docs.
- Every practice question carries a staged hint (strategy, then first step), a "See a worked example" button that shows the same skill with different numbers, per-answer feedback on multiple choice questions, and a full worked solution. The worked example and the worked solution are show/hide toggles: hiding and re-showing never changes what was dealt, and a separate "Show another example" button inside the example panel is the only way to deal a fresh example (in seeded sets the sequence of examples is reproducible, press by press). The end-of-set summary lists any indicators missed and offers a "Practice what I missed" set. A collapsible reference panel sits at the top of each practice set, carrying the unit's formulas and facts (conversion factors and equivalents, percent and pay formulas, area and surface area formulas, the Pythagorean theorem, the primary trig ratios).
- Teachers can set a seed word or phrase in the builder ("Same problems for everyone"): a shared link carrying that seed gives every student the identical set, same questions, same values, same order, including the worked examples. With no seed, each student gets a fresh random set.
- Any online practice set can be downloaded for Kahoot (.xlsx, in Kahoot's template layout) or Blooket (.csv, in Blooket's template layout) to run the same multiple choice questions as a game, with the math written as readable plain text and a time limit set by question level (20, 30 or 60 seconds). The downloads live on the online practice view only; practice sheets and practice tests are show-your-work print products and do not offer them. Constructed-response questions are not exported, and the app says how many were left out.
- Contexts follow the applied stream: Math at Work workplace and everyday situations, plain short stems, both SI and imperial measures appearing as the indicators demand, and money amounts realistic for Newfoundland and Labrador (HST at 15% where tax applies, with rates stated in the stem where a question depends on them).

## Sources and originality

Items are generated from templates written for this bank. Unit placement and indicator text are governed by the official Applied Mathematics 1202 units and indicators spreadsheet (96 unique indicators, codes MA1202.<target>.<indicator>; the app shows the number without the MA1202 prefix, following the junior apps' convention). The public curriculum viewer's indicator listings (96 unique across 117 listings) reconcile exactly with the spreadsheet. The practice-test shape comes from the Mathematics 1202 development shell's unit tests, used as a structural model only, and the shell's end-of-lesson practice catalogue informed item types and lesson topics (Unit 6, the thinnest in the shell, is deliberately fuller here). No question text, scenario, or values from the shell pools, the viewer, or any other source are reproduced, and the app does not mirror the actual unit tests item for item.

Reference panels: no official Math 1202 formula sheet has been located. The per-unit panels are built from the course's own content and are reference panels only; they are not an official formula sheet. Flagged on the QA spot-check list.

Levels: no table of specifications is published for this course. The bank's level mix (about 20% Level 1, 60% Level 2, 20% Level 3; actual whole-bank census 19.8 / 61.0 / 19.2) follows the 20/60/20 split used across the other courses as a design prior, not a specification.
